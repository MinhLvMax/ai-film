Title: VOYAGER 1 Câu chuyện về 10 nghìn tỷ năm tiếp theo _ Space Documentary
**From Launch to Interstellar Frontier**

Born in 1977 for a Saturn mission, Voyager 1 carried a plaque and a suite of instruments that would outpace the solar system. It swung past Jupiter and Saturn, then their icy moons, before breaking the heliosphere at 21,000 km from Earth, cruising 17 km/s into interstellar plasma—humanity can leave the Sun’s bubble.

Now drifting 140 AU beyond the Sun, Voyager 1 still beams faint radio whispers, particle counts, and magnetic field data that map the interstellar medium’s density, turbulence, and composition. These measurements guide probes that could travel, relay messages back to Earth, and turn our record into a bridge toward an explorer network.
